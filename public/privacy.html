Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

This policy explains how Munchy's Grill handles information when you use our website and official iPhone or Android app.

Information in the Munchy's app

The app does not require a Munchy's account. Favorites are stored locally on your device. We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal information through the app.

Menu and ordering

The app retrieves restaurant menu information through our secure server. When you choose to order, checkout opens Toast's hosted ordering service. Toast processes the order, contact details, delivery or pickup details, and payment information under its own terms and privacy practices. Munchy's does not receive or store your full payment-card number through this app.

Website and server logs

Our hosting and security providers may process standard technical information, such as IP address, device or browser type, request time, and error logs, to deliver and protect the service.

Third-party services

The website and app may open Toast for ordering, Google or Apple Maps for directions, and your phone app for calls. Information you provide to those services is governed by their policies.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for general restaurant customers and are not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The effective date above will show the latest revision.
